POS Read-Only Cash Opening



Features
  • Modify the Cash Opening Popup in Odoo POS to make the Opening Cash field read-only.
  • Support Community and Enterprise Editions
Benefits
  • Restrict manual editing of the Opening Cash field in the POS Cash Opening Popup.
  • Ensure that the starting balance of the cash register is pre-filled and immutable during the cash opening process.
  • Simplify the cash opening process by preventing accidental changes to the opening cash amount.
