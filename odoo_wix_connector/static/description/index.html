What is Wix?

Wix is a simple and free website creation tool. It creates professional websites with ease that look fantastic on any screen because of user-friendly technology and powerful built-in features. But Wix is more than just a website.

Presenting Odoo Wix Connector for you!

Odoo Wix Connector for Multichannel helps you to connect your professional eCommerce website to your Odoo.

If you are managing the front end on Wix and want to manage the back end on Odoo ERP, then this module will help you integrate your Wix website information into the Odoo backend.

While managing your E-commerce using Wix, you can also use the backend of Odoo to handle the numerous actions for your Wix E-Commerce Store. combines the greatest features of the two platforms.
