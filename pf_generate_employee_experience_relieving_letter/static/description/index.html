Generate Employee Experience Relieving Letter in Odoo

Our all-inclusive Odoo – Generate Employee Experience Relieving Letter module enables the generation of professionally formatted Employee Experience and Relieving Certificates using customizable templates within Odoo. Designed specifically for A4 landscape paper format, it allows HR teams to create polished, print-ready PDF documents that include dynamic content such as employee name, job title, and employment dates. Templates with custom background designs (such as gold-gray or dark blue-white) can be selected through system settings, ensuring flexibility and consistency with company branding. The module leverages Odoo’s QWeb reporting engine to deliver clean, visually appealing certificates ideal for official use.

  • Choose from professionally designed templates to match your company branding.

Complete Workflow for Generate Employee Experience Relieving Letter in odoo

Configure Template Settings – Go to Employee Settings and Select the desired certificate that matches your company branding. Launch Certificate Wizard – Open the employee profile in the Employee module. Click on Generate Certificate. A wizard will open where you can confirm or edit details like employment dates, designation, etc. Generate and Download Certificate – After filling the wizard, click Print. The system creates a professional, print-ready PDF certificate using the selected template, ready for download or printing.
