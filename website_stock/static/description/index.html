Odoo Website Product Stock

Show stock information to customers in Odoo!

Odoo Website Product Stock allows you to show product stock information such as in-stock, out-of-stock, and last-in-stock. This module enables customers to see the available quantity of products while purchasing. Also, it restricts the customers from adding more than the available quantity to the cart.

Why Do You Need Odoo Website Product Stock?

The module helps you save your customer from the disappointment of going through product details to find out they are out of stock. Additionally, it enables you to create a product reputation that it is a quality product. This will boost buyers' confidence and increases product demand.
This module also allows you to create urgency by showing 'Low stock' that influences the buyer to make a quick decision and purchase immediately before the stock ends.
Benefits of Odoo Website Product Stock

The module has several benefits that help increase your sales. Some of its benefits are

  • It allows you to inform the customer about product stock.
  • It enables you to show various messages such as in-stock, out-of-stock, limited stock, etc.
  • Along with custom messages, it also lets you show stock information so the buyers are aware of the product stock.
  • It influences customers to make faster decisions about a product purchase.

Detailed Features List

Display Product Stock to Website Visitors

  • Odoo Website Product Stock allows you to show stock to registered users and visitors.
  • Users don't need to log in to their accounts to view product stock.

Enter Custom Message for Product Stock

  • With this module, you can add custom messages for in-stock and out-of-stock messages.
  • Gives the option to allow/forbid placing orders for out-of-stock products.

Set Custom Messages for Any Product

  • Allows you to set a custom message for any particular product for stock status.
  • You can also select the stock type to show on the Odoo website.

Overrides default Odoo Functionality

  • Enables you to override the default functionality of Odoo.
  • Allows you to restrict customers from ordering out-of-stock products.
